$ kubectl rollout history rs/myapp-rs        # a bare ReplicaSet
error: no history viewer has been implemented for "ReplicaSet.apps"

  That error IS the answer: a ReplicaSet only keeps N pods alive.
  It has no revisions, no rollout, no undo. A Deployment manages
  ReplicaSets and adds the versioning on top - which is why you
  never write a bare ReplicaSet in production.
