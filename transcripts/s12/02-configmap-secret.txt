  base64 is ENCODING, not encryption. Anyone with get-secret
  rights reads it in one line. RBAC is the actual protection.

# the trailing-newline bug:
$ echo "pass" | base64        vs        echo -n "pass" | base64
  cGFzcwo=   vs   cGFzcw==
  echo adds \n, so the app gets "pass\n" and auth fails.
